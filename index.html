Area Agencies on Aging

https://mn.gov/board-on-aging/about-us/area-agencies/

Area Agencies on Aging (AAAs) are regional organizations that provide the services, supports and information for just about everything that older adults and their families could need. Minnesota has seven AAAs across the state that are the local connections to older Minnesotans and the community support they need as they age.

Minnesota Senior LinkAge Line®

https://mn.gov/senior-linkage-line/

The Minnesota Senior LinkAge Line® is your one-stop resource for aging services and information in Minnesota. Whether you're looking for assistance with Medicare, long-term care options, or caregiver support, Senior LinkAge Line® offers expert guidance and connects you to local resources tailored to your needs.

Planning Ahead for Your Future

https://mn.gov/ooltc/assets/Planning%20Ahead%20Booklet_tcm1168-524041.pdf

Discover the 'Planning Ahead' booklet from Minnesota's Department of Human Services. It provides crucial guidance on long-term care, Medicare, Medicaid, and aging services. Download now to plan ahead confidently.

ASSISTED LIVING Information for Consumers, Families, and Caregivers

https://www.health.state.mn.us/facilities/regulation/assistedliving/consumers.html

Explore the Minnesota Department of Health's Assisted Living Consumer Information website for essential resources and guidance on assisted living options, regulations, and resident rights in Minnesota. Empower yourself with the information you need to make informed decisions.

Office of Ombudsman for Long-Term Care

https://mn.gov/ooltc/residentandfamilyresources/resources/

The Office of Ombudsman for Long-Term Care serves as a vital advocate for residents of nursing homes, assisted living facilities, and other long-term care settings in Minnesota. They provide confidential assistance to resolve concerns, protect resident rights, and ensure quality care. Visit their website to learn more about their services and how they can support you or your loved ones in navigating long-term care.

Frequently Asked Questions for Consumers about Home Care

https://www.health.state.mn.us/facilities/regulation/homecare/consumers/faq.html

Visit the Minnesota Department of Health's Home Care Consumer FAQ webpage for clear answers and essential information on home care options, regulations, and services in Minnesota. Empower yourself with knowledge to make informed decisions.

Moving Checklist for Assisted Living

https://www.asccare.com/caregiver_support/moving-checklist-for-assisted-living/

Explore the moving checklist for assisted living provided by American Senior Communities (ASC). This resource offers practical guidance and tips to help make the transition to assisted living smoother. Whether you're preparing for yourself or a loved one, this checklist covers essential steps from planning and packing to settling in.
